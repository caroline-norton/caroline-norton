Philosophy

Why understory?

In a forest, an underground network connects the root systems of separate trees, moving water, nutrients, and stress signals between them, even between different species. Above ground, neighbouring tree crowns stop just short of touching, called crown shyness, leaving gaps that let light reach the forest floor.

Understory is built on the core principles of shared infrastructure below, and respected space above.

Meet Caroline

Hi, I'm Caroline.

I am an HR professional based in South Africa with experience that spans consulting, coaching, organisational development, and HR strategy.

My corporate expertise includes managing complex HR projects and developing HR strategy, leadership development and HR process redesign. I have worked in complex corporate environments as well as the NGO and small business domain.

I have helped many students, graduates, and professionals achieve their goals by providing them with resourceful insight on how to construct an impactful CV and perform well in interviews. My coaching is based on extensive experience in HR and my deep interest in people, and how to encourage their potential.

With a fervent belief that small, consistent changes in preparation, practice, and systems coaching can maximise your individual and the company's potential, I bring an honest approach to help you navigate your career and workplace goals.

I believe that a consistent approach, in how you initiate, prepare, practise, and build your systems, can make a big difference to both individual and business success. Through practical coaching and guidance, I help you navigate your career, workplace or company's goals with clarity and confidence.
