Talk to us

Root. Canopy. Union.

No two clients need the same thing, so I don't sell fixed packages. Below is the range I work across, and we'll shape the right combination in a first conversation.

Root

People strategy

The long-term, mostly invisible structural work: the foundations everything else depends on.

A root system takes years to establish. It holds a plant upright and carries water and nutrients to every part of it, all without ever being seen.

Running a small business can be challenging on many fronts: from employee engagement to HR management systems. Small businesses often require bespoke, small-scale, adaptable solutions. I work as your HR partner to provide insight, tailored strategy, and structure.

  • Implementing simple and effective strategies and plans around employee experience: development plans, weekly and monthly interlocks, succession planning, and high-level HR management systems, that help your business operate more efficiently.

I partner with you as your HR support, offering tailored strategies to strengthen your people practices, and a smoother, more efficient way to manage your team and grow your business.

Canopy

Strategic resource management

Deciding where scarce resources (time, attention, and room to grow) get allocated across your people.

A canopy is where a forest allocates its most valuable resource. Sunlight reaches some branches and not others, and that uneven distribution shapes how the whole tree grows.

Having an impartial sounding board is one of the most important and underrated tools a person can use in navigating the world of work, whether you are a recent graduate or an experienced employee.

Dealing with conflict, entering into difficult conversations, and exploring career options are part of career guidance and coaching: deciding, deliberately, who gets space to grow and how. Let's explore what your needs are.

Union

Matching people to roles

Precise fit for both sides of a hire: the candidate presenting themselves accurately, and the hiring team choosing well.

A union is the point where two separate plants are joined so closely that they grow as one. It holds only when both sides were suited to each other from the start.

For candidates

CV optimisation

CV coaching sessions help you expand and rework your CV to create relevant and impactful content that captures the essence of you, your skills and expertise. Your CV is often your first opportunity to make an impression. It is really important.

Getting your CV right is an iterative process and will require self-reflection, honesty and a bit of time.

For candidates

Interview preparation

Interviews can be scary and many people can get themselves into panic mode. Nerves can trip you up, and sometimes you just aren't sure how to showcase your best self in an interview.

Interview preparation and practice can develop your interview skills as well as deftness in answering tricky questions. It involves general discussion and discovery, role playing, practice, and coaching.

For hiring teams

Recruitment & interview strategy

Making the wrong hiring decision can be detrimental, and the cost is amplified in small businesses. I help you refine and develop your recruitment and interview process, so the fit is right on both sides from the start.
